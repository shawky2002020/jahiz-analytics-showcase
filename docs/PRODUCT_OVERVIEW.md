# Product overview

Jahiz Analytics is a mobile-first product for structured karate match tracking and performance review. It is designed for coaches, athletes, teams, squads, and competition participants who need more than informal post-match notes.

The product follows a practical flow: create and manage the relevant athlete/player context, organize an individual or supported team match, record scoring and penalty activity, complete and review the match, then use verified analytics to guide follow-up.

This showcase does not describe private implementation details, internal service topology, account controls, or commercial configuration.
