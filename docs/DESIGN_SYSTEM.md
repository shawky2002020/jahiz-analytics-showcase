# Design system

The showcase takes its direction from Jahiz’s established product palette: near-black surfaces (`#080808` / `#111111`), brand blue (`#3388EE`) as the dominant color, controlled red (`#E74C3C`) as a live-match accent, and high-contrast neutral typography.

The visual direction is precise and athletic rather than decorative: editorial spacing, sharp information hierarchy, restrained color, and no simulated product screens. Brand imagery uses the official logo after metadata stripping; diagrams communicate product concepts without exposing architecture.
